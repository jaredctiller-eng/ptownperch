Section 10Occupancy Rules

Each of the following is a material term of this Agreement.

  • No smoking or vaping of any substance anywhere on the Property, including the garden, windows, and doorways. Violation incurs a remediation charge of $500.
  • No parties, events, or gatherings. The Property may not be used for any commercial purpose, photography or filming, or any event beyond the registered occupants.
  • Quiet hours from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM. The Property adjoins occupied residences and sits above operating businesses. Guest agrees to keep noise in the unit, the garden, and on Commercial Street considerate at all times.
  • Condominium rules. The Property is subject to the rules and regulations of the condominium association, provided in the house manual. Guest is responsible for any fine, assessment, or penalty levied against Owner as a result of Guest's conduct.
  • No pets. No animal of any kind may be brought to or kept at the Property at any time, including by visitors and including animals left unattended in a vehicle on the premises. Bringing an undisclosed animal is a material breach, incurs a charge of $500 for allergen remediation and deep cleaning, and is grounds for immediate termination of the stay without refund under Section 14. Guest is additionally liable for any damage the animal causes and for any claim, fine, or assessment arising from its presence.
  • Service animals. The no-pet policy does not apply to a service animal that Owner is required by applicable law to accommodate. Guest shall notify Owner prior to arrival if a service animal will accompany Guest. A service animal must be under Guest's control at all times, must not be left unattended at the Property, and Guest remains fully responsible and liable for any damage or injury it causes, including under Massachusetts law imposing liability on the keeper of a dog. An animal that is not a service animal within the meaning of applicable law — including an animal described as an emotional support, comfort, or therapy animal — is a pet for purposes of this Agreement.
  • No subletting or reassignment of the reservation.
  • Parking is not provided. The Property includes no parking space of any kind. Public municipal parking is available in Provincetown at Guest’s own expense and on a first-come basis. Guest is solely responsible for locating parking and for all parking fees, tickets, and towing costs. Owner makes no representation that parking will be available.
  • Trash and recycling must be sorted and placed as described in the house manual, per Town of Provincetown collection rules.
  • Locking up. Guest shall lock all doors and windows when absent.
  • Prohibited: candles, open flame, space heaters, grills, fireworks, firearms, drones, and illegal substances.
  • Compliance with law. Guest and all occupants shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and Town of Provincetown laws and ordinances. Any illegal activity terminates this Agreement immediately.
